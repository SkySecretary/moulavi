Required Documents & Checklist

Ensure you have these four vital components ready. Our portal allows bulk uploads and one-click submissions for all your pilgrims.

1️⃣
Mandatory

Nusuk Booking

Book your pilgrim's Umrah slot through the official Nusuk App in accordance with their designated travel date and itinerary.

Slot timing must align with arrival in Kingdom
2️⃣
Flight Data

Travel Details ✈️

Provide confirmed Up & Down travel details including flight numbers, entry port (JED/MED), arrival date/time, and departure flight schedules.

Complete return journey confirmation required
3️⃣
Existing Visa

Visa Copy 📄

Send a clear copy of the pilgrim's existing Umrah Visa that was previously issued through another company or agency.

High-resolution PDF or Image copy
4️⃣
Accommodation

BRNS / Hotel Booking 🏨

Provide valid BRNS (Booking Reference Numbers) or hotel booking confirmations covering the complete period of stay across Makkah and Madinah.

Continuous stay coverage without gaps

Frequently Asked Questions

Umrah Re-Entry Visa FAQs

Can we apply if the pilgrim's initial visa was issued by another company?

Yes, absolutely! The Re-Entry Visa service is specifically designed to assist travel agents and pilgrims whose initial visa was processed via another provider. You only need to supply the existing visa copy along with the 4 required details.

How fast will the re-entry visa assistance be completed?

Once all 4 requirements (Nusuk slot, Flight tickets, Visa Copy, and BRN) are verified, our operations team processes the gateway submission within rapid turnaround time.

What if the pilgrim does not have a BRN yet?

BRN hotel coverage is a mandatory requirement for Nusuk compliance. If you need hotel reservations or BRN assistance, you can coordinate directly with our operations desk via WhatsApp or the portal.

How do existing B2B agents initiate a Re-Entry booking?

Simply log in to the Agent Portal, navigate to "Umrah Visa > Re-Entry Visa", and submit the passenger and travel data directly.
